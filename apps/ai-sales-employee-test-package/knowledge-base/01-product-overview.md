## What it is
An AI-powered virtual sales representative that real estate companies use to hold the first sales conversations with their leads, by voice, in natural Egyptian Arabic or English. It contacts the company's authorised leads, answers common questions from the company's approved information, qualifies interest against the company's own criteria, and hands promising opportunities to the company's human sales team.

## Who it is for
Real estate developers, brokerages and property marketing companies that receive more leads than their sales team can call promptly, and that want consistent first contact and qualification.
Typical decision makers: owners, CEOs and managing directors, sales directors and managers, business development managers, lead generation and customer acquisition managers.

## Problems it can help with
- Leads that wait too long for a first call, or never get one.
- Sales consultants spending a large share of their time on repetitive first calls and the same basic questions.
- Inconsistent qualification: different consultants ask different questions and record different things.
- Follow-up that depends on individual discipline.
- Little visibility into what happened on first calls.

## How it works (high level)
1. The real estate company provides eligible leads (with a lawful basis to contact them) and approved information about its projects: descriptions, prices, payment plans and FAQs.
2. The AI Sales Employee calls authorised leads through the configured calling system, within the company's calling rules.
3. It holds a natural sales conversation using only the approved knowledge base.
4. It identifies interest and qualifies leads against agreed criteria.
5. It produces a structured call outcome and summary for each call.
6. Qualified opportunities are passed to the company's human sales team.
7. Planned (not yet available): delivering follow-ups and lead records by email, CRM or Google Sheets, and WhatsApp.

## AI-led first conversation, human-led closing
The AI handles the first conversation, answers standard questions, and qualifies. Human consultants handle negotiation, high-value decisions, complex questions, and closing. The aim is to support the sales team and free their time for the conversations that need a person, not to replace them.
