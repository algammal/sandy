# Capabilities: Current vs Planned

> Rule for the assistant: only describe items under CURRENT as things the product does today. Items under PLANNED must always be called "planned" or "on our roadmap", never "available". Never give a delivery date for planned items.

## CURRENT (implemented and tested in our own setup)
These run on our current platform. Each client still needs setup: their phone line, knowledge and calling rules.
- **Natural voice conversations** in Egyptian Arabic, with English available for prospects who prefer it.
- **Transparent AI disclosure:** it always introduces itself as an AI assistant.
- **Answers from approved knowledge only:** it uses the client's verified project information and says so when it doesn't know, instead of guessing.
- **Lead qualification** against configurable criteria: interest, need, timeline and agreed next step, with categories hot, warm, cold, not interested, do-not-call, unreachable, and human follow-up required.
- **Objection handling** for common questions, without pressure and without inventing offers.
- **Do-not-call handling:** a request to stop calling is honoured immediately and the number is suppressed from future calls.
- **Calling safeguards:** consent and eligibility checks before each call, calling-hours windows, retry limits and duplicate-call prevention.
- **Structured call outcomes and summaries** after each call.
- **Hand-off requests:** it captures when and how the client's human sales team should follow up.

## PLANNED (not available yet)
- **CRM or Google Sheets integration:** automatically creating and updating lead records.
- **Email follow-up:** sending brochures, summaries and meeting confirmations.
- **WhatsApp follow-up:** sending approved information and meeting details, with the customer's opt-in.
- **Automatic meeting booking:** booking demos or viewings directly into a calendar.
- **Live transfer** of a call to a human consultant.
- **Reporting dashboard.**

Until these are live, follow-up details are recorded and the sales team acts on them.
