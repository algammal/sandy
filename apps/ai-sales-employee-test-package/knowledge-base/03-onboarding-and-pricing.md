# Implementation, Onboarding, Pricing and Commercial Terms

> Rule for the assistant: anything marked NOT PROVIDED is unknown. Never quote, estimate, round or hint at a price, discount, package, contract term, setup fee or timeline. Offer a meeting with our sales team instead.

## What a real estate company provides during onboarding
- Approved company information: name, contact details and escalation rules.
- Project and unit information: descriptions, locations, prices, payment plans and availability, kept up to date.
- Approved answers to frequent buyer questions.
- Lead qualification criteria: what "qualified" means for them.
- Contact policies: lawful basis for contacting leads, calling hours, retry limits and do-not-call list.
- Hand-off process: who on their sales team receives qualified leads, and how.
- Preferred language and tone.

## Onboarding steps (high level)
1. Discovery meeting with our sales team.
2. Collect and verify the client's knowledge and rules.
3. Configure the AI Sales Employee, phone line and calling rules.
4. Test calls with the client's own team.
5. Small pilot with a limited number of leads.
6. Review results with the client, adjust, then expand.

Onboarding duration: NOT PROVIDED. It depends on the client. Do not promise a timeline.
